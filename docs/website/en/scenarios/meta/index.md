# Business Scenarios

This documentation set showcases the PowerX platform's scenario-based use case designs across various business domains, covering complete business blueprints from user requirements to system implementation.

## Business Domains

### 🧩 PowerX Platform
- **Path**: [PowerX Scenarios](./powerx/)
- **Coverage**: Agent orchestration, plugin ecosystem, core platform, marketplace operations, etc.

### 🔌 Plugin Ecosystem
- **Path**: [Plugin Development & Distribution](./plugin/)
- **Coverage**: Plugin local debugging, offline packaging, online publishing, etc.

### 🛒 Ecommerce
- **Path**: [Ecommerce Scenarios](./ecommerce/)
- **Coverage**: Order fulfillment, payment settlement, inventory management, membership marketing, risk & compliance, etc.

### 👥 Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
- **Path**: [CRM Scenarios](./crm/)
- **Coverage**: Customer management, sales processes, etc.

### 🔄 Social Customer Relationship Management (SCRM)
- **Path**: [SCRM Scenarios](./scrm/)
- **Coverage**: Social marketing, customer engagement, etc.

## Documentation Structure

Each business domain follows a three-tier structure:
> **Category → Primary Use Case → Scenario Use Case**

Each scenario follows a narrative style of "Role + Trigger + Behavior + Goal" to ensure authenticity in business scenarios.
