# Marketing Automation

Marketing Automation focuses on campaign orchestration, channel execution, lead nurturing, scoring, and continuous conversion optimization.

## Sub-scenarios

- **[Campaign Orchestration & Management](./campaign-orchestration-management/primary.md)** – design visual workflows, control budget and cadence, and automate post-campaign reviews.
- **[Content & Channel Outreach](./content-channel-outreach/primary.md)** – manage templates, schedule multi-channel delivery, and keep approvals compliant.
- **[Lead Nurturing](./lead-nurturing/primary.md)** – automate journeys, serve personalized content, and coordinate manual touchpoints.
- **[Lead Scoring & Assignment](./lead-scoring-assignment/primary.md)** – run scoring models, route hot leads intelligently, and monitor fairness.
- **[Testing & Conversion Optimization](./testing-conversion-optimization/primary.md)** – perform A/B tests, analyze funnels, and roll out winning variants.
