# Communication & Collaboration

The Communication & Collaboration module centrally manages customer communication, task collaboration, and channel partner collaboration around social touchpoints like WeCom, ensuring information transparency and timely responses.

## Sub-scenarios

- **[Omnichannel Communication Logging](./omnichannel-communication-logging/primary.md)** - Unified call, email, and meeting record logging
- **[Task & Cadence Collaboration](./task-cadence-collaboration/primary.md)** - Lead task assignment, cross-department collaboration, and reminders
- **[Channel & Partner Collaboration](./channel-partner-collaboration/primary.md)** - Partner progress sync, change request approval, and performance settlement
