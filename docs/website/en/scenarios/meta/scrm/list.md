# 🧩 PowerX SCRM Scenario-based Use Case List

*(Scenario-based Use Case List for PowerXSCRMPlugin — Based on WeCom ecosystem, extensible to other social platforms)*

---

## I. Social Channel Onboarding & Governance

### Primary Use Case: WeCom Account & Permission Management

* Operations integrates new business departments into WeCom, synchronizing members and tags based on organizational structure.
* Security team regularly audits external contact permissions to prevent departed employees from accessing customer data.
* Super admin authorizes API permissions for third-party applications, with the system recording approval and audit logs.
* When a company changes social platforms or adds sub-accounts, the system automatically syncs to the SCRM workspace.

### Primary Use Case: Unified Social Channel Access

* Marketing operations integrates WeCom, video channels, public accounts and other touchpoints to manage messages and customer profiles uniformly.
* System detects abnormal channel connection status, automatically alerts and provides repair guidance.
* Data team configures channel attribution parameters to achieve lead source tracking.
* When new channels go live, SCRM automatically generates integration environments and test accounts.

---

## II. System Integration & Data Orchestration

### Primary Use Case: CRM & SCRM Bidirectional Sync

* New or updated social traffic leads are automatically written to CRM, maintaining the same customer ID.
* CRM opportunity status changes (won/lost) are instantly written back to WeCom, reminding front-line teams to adjust follow-up cadence.
* When customer success changes the owner in CRM, SCRM automatically completes friend transfer and group permission handover.
* Data team validates sync logs in the data middle platform, supporting replay and compensation when abnormalities occur.

### Primary Use Case: Order & Fulfillment Event Relay

* After the order system updates shipping status, WeCom robots automatically notify customers to view logistics information.
* Refund or after-sales progress is synced to customer conversation windows, ensuring information transparency.
* When important orders are delayed, the system triggers internal group alerts for sales and customer service collaboration.
* After order completion, return visit tasks or review invitations are automatically generated, closing the transaction experience.

### Primary Use Case: Membership & Entitlement Sync

* When membership center upgrades member levels, WeCom tags are instantly updated, linked with community operation strategies.
* Points, entitlement packages, and stored value balances are synced in real-time between CRM and WeCom to avoid duplicate redemptions.
* Membership entitlement changes trigger automatic message pushes, reminding customers to claim new benefits.
* The business rules platform records the source system and operator for each entitlement sync for auditing purposes.

### Primary Use Case: Inventory & Recommendation Orchestration

* When ERP inventory drops below threshold, notify social sales teams to adjust recommendation strategies.
* After new products arrive, SCRM pushes product updates and trial invitations based on customer preferences.
* When regional warehouse inventory is sufficient, the system automatically adjusts community activity priorities and discount levels.
* Data analysis links inventory turnover with social conversion to form dynamic recommendation models.

---

## III. Lead Capture & Smart Assignment

### Primary Use Case: Social Lead Capture & Deduplication

* After users scan QR codes to join live codes, the system automatically identifies duplicate customers and archives interaction history.
* Social fission links generated by marketing activities transmit leads in real-time, with data written back to CRM.
* When visitors submit requirement forms in WeCom mini-programs, lead grading strategies are automatically triggered.
* When lead quality scores are too low, the system suggests adding to nurture journeys rather than direct sales assignment.

### Primary Use Case: Lead Routing & Reception Strategy

* WeCom channel leads are intelligently assigned to designated sales or customer service by region and industry.
* Reception rules support "prioritize original follower" to ensure consistent customer experience.
* When busy, switch to robot reception and transfer to human when available for continued follow-up.
* Supervisors can view lead assignment dashboards in real-time, adjusting strategies or temporarily taking over.

---

## IV. Community & Customer Engagement

### Primary Use Case: Customer Group Operations & Profiling

* Operations creates WeCom groups to guide customers to join, with the system automatically recording group member identity and join channels.
* Real-time tagging of interaction behaviors in groups helps identify potential customers and key accounts.
* When group owners leave or transfer positions, the system automatically transfers group management permissions and preserves chat records.
* Machine assistants regularly push group operation reminders, suggesting content and optimal timing.

### Primary Use Case: Social Customer Lifecycle Management

* New group members trigger welcome journeys, with robots sending tiered content and guiding first purchase completion.
* Active customers enter the membership growth system, with WeCom tags and CRM levels automatically synced.
* Long-term silent customers are automatically added to awakening plans, with operations able to view execution results and conversion.
* High-value customer interaction results on social channels are written back to CRM, forming complete second-party profiles.

---

## V. Smart Tagging & Customer Segmentation

### Primary Use Case: Automated Tagging & Scoring

* System automatically applies activity and intent tags based on interaction frequency, spending ability, and feedback sentiment.
* New tags automatically record sources (behavioral, manual, external data) to avoid duplicate construction.
* Tag validity and decay policies are automatically executed to ensure continuous accuracy of customer profiles.
* Operations can view tag additions, coverage, and effectiveness on dashboards.

### Primary Use Case: Dynamic Segmentation & RFM Insights

* RFM model automatically divides customers into high-value, potential, dormant, and other groups.
* Segmentation rules support real-time refresh by behavioral dimensions (recent interactions, channel preferences).
* Segmentation results can be directly synced to WeCom communities, content journeys, and CRM marketing campaigns.
* Operations adjust strategies based on segmentation performance, tracking conversion and repurchase data.

### Primary Use Case: Tag Conflict & Weight Governance

* When a customer simultaneously has "price-sensitive" and "high AOV" tags, the system automatically determines the primary tag based on weights.
* Tag conflict rules support configurable priority, mutual exclusion relationships, and source credibility.
* Regular tag conflict reports remind operations to adjust strategies or clean redundant tags.
* Risk control scenarios can forcibly override tags to ensure compliance priorities are highest.

### Primary Use Case: Tag-driven Activation Use Cases

* Automatically match sales scripts and recommended products based on tags to improve conversion rates.
* Membership benefits, coupons, and course resources are precisely distributed based on tags.
* Customer service can view tags in real-time during conversations, quickly understanding customer preferences and historical requests.
* Data teams sync tags to BI tools for comparative analysis with offline channel data.

---

## VI. Content Distribution & Engagement Automation

### Primary Use Case: WeCom Content Hub & Campaign Orchestration

* Content operations upload graphic and video assets to the asset library, uniformly managing versions and approval records.
* Automated processes push personalized content based on customer tags and track read/click performance.
* Before live streams or events start, the system sends reminder messages and registration links according to time schedules.
* Content publishing supports A/B testing, with different groups receiving different scripts to compare conversions.

### Primary Use Case: Chatbot Interaction & Messaging Automation

* Robots automatically reply to common questions, identify keywords, then transfer to human handling.
* When users consult at night, trigger "night customer service mode" pushing FAQs and self-service portals.
* After customers fill out questionnaires, robots generate summaries and sync to responsible colleagues.
* Abnormal messages (such as accidentally sending sensitive words) can be recalled or replaced, with the system recording compliance logs.

---

## VII. Social Commerce & Distribution

### Primary Use Case: WeCom Mini Program Commerce

* Customers can directly open mini-programs to place orders in chat interfaces, with orders automatically linked to initiating employees.
* The system calculates commissions and incentives according to rules, providing real-time feedback to sales and their supervisors.
* Order exceptions (out of stock, payment failures) are notified to customers and customer service via WeCom for handling.
* After data returns, automatically trigger after-sales care and repurchase recommendations.

### Primary Use Case: Group Payment & Coupon Redemption

* Initiate group buying or flash sales in groups, with customers able to pay directly and view progress.
* Coupons and stored value cards can be redeemed with one click in chat windows, automatically updating balances and entitlements.
* After payment completion, push electronic invoices or receipts to ensure transaction compliance.
* Operations analyze group payment conversion rates to optimize activity strategies.

### Primary Use Case: Personalized Social Commerce Recommendations

* System dynamically recommends products or services based on tags, purchase history, and inventory status.
* Customers see "personalized for you" product cards when opening conversations, achieving thousand-people-thousand-faces.
* Operations can A/B test recommendation strategies, observing add-to-cart and conversion performance across different groups.
* Recommendation logic is solidified into strategy templates that can be reused in live streams, offline events, etc.

### Primary Use Case: Social Affiliate & Partner Selling

* Customers can apply to become distribution partners, with the system automatically verifying qualifications and binding relationships.
* Partners share products or live codes via WeCom, with the system tracking referral effects and commissions.
* Performance rankings and reward distribution are transparently displayed in WeCom to encourage continued referral.
* Distribution risks (abnormal returns, order manipulation) are monitored in real-time by risk control strategies.

## VIII. Social Selling Assistant & Field Collaboration

### Primary Use Case: Social Selling Cadence

* Sales use WeCom external contact features to view customer profiles and historical interactions, developing outreach plans.
* System recommends optimal contact times and script templates based on customer behavior.
* After sales field visits, mobile reminders sync meeting minutes and update opportunity status.
* At key nodes, trigger supervisor accompaniment or multi-party collaboration to ensure high-value opportunities aren't missed.

### Primary Use Case: Social Referral & Campaign Execution

* Marketing generates referral posters or live codes. After sales share them, the system tracks referral effects.
* Daily summaries of sales social outreach frequency, live code conversion rates, and activity registrations.
* After successful transactions, automatically distribute referral rewards, with sales and customer success teams able to view in real-time.
* When referral activities perform poorly, the system suggests adjusting poster copy or reward strategies.

## IX. Customer Service & Collaboration Loop

### Primary Use Case: WeCom Service Desk & Ticket Sync

* When customers consult via WeCom, tickets are automatically generated and assigned to corresponding customer service.
* Customer service convert chat records into knowledge base cases to improve subsequent problem handling efficiency.
* When difficult issues need escalation, the system syncs to the ticketing system, allowing product and development collaboration on the same platform.
* Service results and satisfaction scores sync back to CRM/SCRM, forming a service closed loop.

### Primary Use Case: CS & Ops Collaboration

* Customer success managers proactively send usage reports in chats, with customers able to view with one click.
* When customer service encounter upsell opportunities, they can directly create opportunities in chat interfaces and assign sales for follow-up.
* After operations post announcements in groups, automatically count read confirmations, reminding unread customers to catch up.
* Customer feedback issues are automatically aggregated into product requirement pools, helping teams prioritize optimization.

## X. Analytics & Insights

### Primary Use Case: Social Touchpoint Dashboard

* Operations view WeCom friend growth, group activity, content read, and other metrics.
* Sales managers analyze outreach frequency and conversion rates to optimize human resource investment.
* Marketing evaluates referral activity ROI, identifying efficient channels and activity types.
* Support multi-dimensional drilling (employee, department, region, activity) to find best practices.

### Primary Use Case: Customer Profile & Behavioral Insights

* System integrates social interactions, e-commerce orders, and customer service records to generate 360° customer profiles.
* Predictive models identify at-risk customers, generating retention suggestions and execution plans.
* Analyze customer preferences through social behavior heatmaps, providing basis for content and product iteration.
* Data insight reports support exporting de-identified versions for cross-departmental sharing.

## XI. Mobile Frontline Capabilities

### Primary Use Case: Location Check-in & Visit Tracking

* When sales visit customers, they can check in with one tap in WeCom, with the system automatically recording time, location, and customer name.
* After visits end, automatically generate meeting minute templates, reminding to fill key matters and next steps.
* Supervisors can view team field distribution via map views and coordinate resources in time.
* Abnormal trajectories (such as location deviations) are automatically reported, improving field management transparency.

### Primary Use Case: Voice-to-Text & Insight Extraction

* Sales send voice messages on mobile, with the system providing real-time transcription and extracting customer intent information.
* Transcription results automatically sync to CRM/SCRM, reducing manual recording.
* Long voice messages can generate summaries for teams to quickly understand communication content.
* Sensitive information is automatically redacted or prompts for re-recording to meet compliance requirements.

### Primary Use Case: Offline Messaging & Alerts

* When networks are unstable, messages are cached locally on mobile and automatically sent after network recovery.
* Offline reminders support important customer messages, ensuring first-response capability.
* System can customize alert levels to avoid information overload.
* Operation logs record message delays and re-sends for troubleshooting.

### Primary Use Case: Mobile Approval & Code-based Operations

* Applications, permission approvals, and other processes can be approved on mobile, reducing processing time.
* Offline activities identify customer identity through QR/NFC scanning, real-time syncing check-in and claim records.
* Scanning material in/out inventory automatically generates registrations, avoiding paper processes.
* Approval nodes support profile integration, allowing approvers to view customer background and history with one tap.

### Primary Use Case: AI-driven Outreach Recommendation

* Sales can directly use AI-generated message templates, with custom personalized statements.
* System records recommendation adoption rates and conversion effects, continuously optimizing models.
* High-potential leads are automatically added to focus lists, prompting supervisors to assist with follow-up.

* Generate QA reports with improvement suggestions, reducing manual sampling costs.

### Primary Use Case: AI Coaching & Playbook Enhancement

* New employees can role-play with AI, simulating customer questions and objection handling.
* AI provides script scores and improvement suggestions, solidifying best practices.
* Training data supports export to provide materials for offline training.
* Supervisors can view team practice situations, providing targeted guidance for weak areas.

### Primary Use Case: Generative Content Acceleration

* AI automatically generates poster copy, community scripts, and short video scripts based on event themes.
* After operations review, can publish to asset library with one tap, shortening creation cycles.
* System records AI-generated content usage and conversion performance, continuously tuning optimization.
* Content approval combines AI risk assessment, warning of potentially policy-violating words or phrases.

## XIII. Platform Ecosystem & Extensibility

### Primary Use Case: Open Platform & Plugin Marketplace

* Third-party developers access via open platform, providing extension plugins for SCRM.
* Plugin listing requires security audits and sandbox testing to ensure compatibility.
* Companies can build custom plugins to quickly meet personalized business needs.
* Plugin usage and performance monitoring are uniformly managed, supporting on-demand deactivation/updates.

### Primary Use Case: Data Hub & External APIs

* SCRM provides customer data, tags, and interaction records to BI tools via APIs.
* Data access supports permissions and de-identification controls to meet compliance requirements.
* Companies can build custom dashboards or data products, connecting more business scenarios.
* API call logs and quota management ensure stability and security.

### Primary Use Case: Workflow Orchestration & Automation

* Visual workflow engine supports complex cross-system process configuration.
* Business personnel can quickly build approval, outreach, and sync strategies through drag-and-drop nodes.
* Process execution logs and monitoring metrics help locate abnormalities and optimize chains.
* Support custom triggers and scripts for highly flexible business orchestration.

### Primary Use Case: Unified SSO & Private Deployment

* Integrate with existing enterprise SSO (DingTalk, Feishu, AD) to achieve single sign-on.
* Support private deployment or hybrid cloud models to meet data sovereignty requirements.
* Security policies (IP whitelisting, multi-factor authentication) are uniformly distributed to all entrances.
* Deployment operations provide automatic updates and patch management, reducing operational complexity.

## XIV. Compliance, Security & Risk Control

### Primary Use Case: Conversation Compliance & Archiving

* Compliance team configures sensitive word libraries, monitoring WeChat chat content in real-time.
* Violating messages trigger automatic review or recall and generate remediation tickets.
* Chat records are regularly archived according to policies, supporting compliance review and auditing.
* De-identification strategies are executed before displaying customer sensitive information to ensure data security.

### Primary Use Case: Account Security & Risk Alerts

* System monitors abnormal behaviors such as abnormal logins and account sharing, locking accounts in time.
* When external contact numbers spike, trigger risk control alerts to prevent rapid data export.
* Compliance checks are performed before mass messaging to avoid violating platform rules.
* Partner access to external applications requires approval, with the system recording and aligning audit tickets.
