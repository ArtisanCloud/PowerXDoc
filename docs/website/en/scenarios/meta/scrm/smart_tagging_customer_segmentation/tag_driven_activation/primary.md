# Primary Use Case: Tag-driven Activation Use Cases

## Background Overview

Customer tags should drive real business actions and decisions. Without activation, tags remain unused data points. This primary use case describes how to activate tags across sales, marketing, and service functions to drive business outcomes.

## Goals & Value
- **Precision Outreach**: Target customers based on tags.
- **Sales Assist**: Help sales teams with tag-based insights.
- **Service Prioritization**: Prioritize service based on customer value.
- **Business Impact**: Drive measurable business outcomes with tags.

## Participating Roles
- **Sales Teams**: Use tags for customer insights and targeting.
- **Marketing**: Activate tags for campaigns and communications.
- **Customer Service**: Prioritize and personalize service.
- **Operations**: Monitor tag activation effectiveness.
- **Management**: Review business impact and ROI.

## Primary Scenario User Story
> **As a** sales representative, **I want** to see customer tags during interactions, **so that** I can personalize my approach and improve my success rate.

## Sub-scenario Details

### Sub-scenario A: Tag-triggered Campaigns
- **Roles & Triggers**: Launch campaigns based on customer tags.
- **Main Process**:
  1. Select target segments based on tags.
  2. Design tag-specific messages and offers.
  3. Launch targeted campaigns automatically.
  4. Track campaign performance by tag segment.
- **Success Criteria**: Effective targeting; relevant messaging; measurable results.
- **Exceptions & Risk Control**: Targeting errors; message relevance; performance tracking.
- **Metric Suggestions: Campaign effectiveness, message relevance, conversion rate.

### Sub-scenario B: Sales Decision Support
- **Roles & Triggers**: Sales need tag-based insights during customer interactions.
- **Main Process**:
  1. Display customer tags in sales interface.
  2. Provide tag-based recommendations and insights.
  3. Suggest next best actions based on tags.
  4. Track sales performance by tag segment.
- **Success Criteria**: Relevant insights; helpful recommendations; improved sales.
- **Exceptions & Risk Control**: Insight relevance; recommendation accuracy; sales impact.
- **Metric Suggestions: Insight utilization, recommendation accuracy, sales improvement.

### Sub-scenario C: Service Prioritization
- **Roles & Triggers**: Need to prioritize service based on customer value.
- **Main Process**:
  1. Classify service priority based on tags.
  2. Route high-value customers to senior agents.
  3. Provide tag-based context to service teams.
  4. Monitor service quality by priority level.
- **Success Criteria**: Proper prioritization; efficient routing; quality service.
- **Exceptions & Risk Control**: Misclassification; routing errors; service quality.
- **Metric Suggestions: Prioritization accuracy, routing efficiency, service quality.

### Sub-scenario D: Cross-functional Activation
- **Roles & Triggers**: Tags should be used across all departments.
- **Main Process**:
  1. Integrate tags into all business systems.
  2. Provide tag-based training to all teams.
  3. Monitor tag usage across functions.
  4. Share best practices and success stories.
- **Success Criteria**: Widespread tag adoption; consistent usage; shared learning.
- **Exceptions & Risk Control**: Adoption barriers; inconsistent usage; silo mentality.
- **Metric Suggestions: Adoption rate, usage consistency, cross-functional impact.

  2. Track campaign performance.
- **Expected Results**:

  - Higher engagement and conversion from tagged segments.
  - Performance improvement tracked and measured.
