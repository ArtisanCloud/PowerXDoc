# PowerX Product Overview

Operations and product teams who steward enterprise automation often juggle fragmented bots, manual approvals and compliance audits. PowerX unifies those efforts so an operations lead can launch a trustworthy workflow within a week, align plugins across teams and keep auditors confident from day one. Treat this page as the narrative starting point for executives, customers and partners exploring how PowerX lands in their world.

## Vision {#vision}

Build the most trusted automation fabric where every enterprise scenario can be orchestrated by collaborative agents, governed end-to-end and iterated in days instead of quarters.

## Positioning {#positioning}

PowerX serves:

- Heads of operations who need governed automation across business units without waiting on engineering queues.
- Integration and platform teams that must modernise plugin ecosystems while keeping security and observability central.
- Solution partners looking to package repeatable industry accelerators on top of a compliant agent runtime.

- **Marketplace Launchpad** – Switch on curated automation packs from the PowerX Marketplace on day one so approvals, notifications and analytics arrive prewired. Explore the [Auto upgrade scenario](/en/scenarios/SCN-OPS-PLUGIN-LIFECYCLE-001/SCN-OPS-PLUGIN-AUTO-UPGRADE-001) to see collaborative agents keeping releases healthy.

## Product Matrix {#product-matrix}

### Core Foundation

**PowerX Core Base**  
Enterprise runtime and governance kernel covering IAM, RBAC, workflow engine, events, auditing and multi-tenant controls.  
**Use it when:** you need a compliant automation backbone that scales across business units.

**PowerX Plugin Scaffold**  
Opinionated toolchain and CLI so teams can scaffold, test and package production-grade plugins in under an hour.  
**Use it when:** you want internal or partner developers to ship extensions without wrestling with infrastructure.

### Marketplace & Extension Hub

**PowerX Plugin Market**  
Curated marketplace orchestrating install, licensing, updates and dependency management for official and third-party plugins.  
**Use it when:** you expect plug-and-play automation packs and life-cycle control from day one.

### Ready-to-Use Suites (Free)

**PowerX CRM**  
Customer operations workspace with pipeline visibility, guided follow-ups and automation.  
**Best for:** go-to-market and account teams getting started immediately.

**PowerX Commerce**  
Catalog, inventory, order and settlement management for multi-store operations.  
**Best for:** commerce and operations teams launching digital storefronts.

**PowerX SCRM**  
Social marketing and private-domain engagement hub with audience segmentation and campaign analytics.  
**Best for:** growth and community teams coordinating multi-channel outreach.

### Professional Add-ons

**PowerX SCRUM**  
Agile delivery cockpit covering backlogs, sprints, boards and velocity insights.  
**Best for:** product and engineering organisations needing deeper execution visibility.

**PowerX Wallet**  
Enterprise wallet and settlement module handling multi-currency reconciliation, cashflow and risk alerts.  
**Best for:** finance and revenue operations overseeing complex settlement flows.

**PowerX MediaX**  
Media asset hub with rights management, tagging and omni-channel content distribution.  
**Best for:** brand, marketing and content studios managing high-volume assets.
